Help & setup

Two minutes before the first game, then you never think about it again.

Put it on your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen from now on. It runs full screen and works with no signal.

The first open needs a signal so it can save itself for offline use. After that, airplane mode is fine.

Lock the phone to this app (Guided Access)

  1. Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access, turn it on. Set a passcode. Turn on Accessibility Shortcut.
  2. Open Go In For, then triple-click the side button and tap Start.
  3. Notifications, other apps, and the Home gesture are blocked until you triple-click again and enter the passcode.

Guided Access keeps other apps out. The Pocket screen in the menu keeps pocket taps out: it shows only the clock and the call, and it unlocks with a one-second hold.

Pre-game checklist

  • Ringer switch on. AirPods in if you want the calls spoken.
  • Screen brightness up. The app keeps the screen awake while the clock runs.
  • Tap the kids who are here. Check the minutes line under the roster.
  • Start the game, triple-click for Guided Access, tap the menu and choose Pocket screen.

During the game

  • One minute before a swap it says who is coming. When the timer hits, it says it again and buzzes.
  • Press and hold Done, they swapped once the kids have moved. It repeats the call every so often until you do.
  • A kid walks off: tap Left on their row. It names the next kid to send in and checks back with you after a few minutes.
  • Tap a kid on the field, then a kid on the bench, to swap by hand. Undo is in the menu.

Known limits

  • The phone screen has to stay on. iOS freezes web apps when the screen locks, so there are no locked-screen alerts.
  • The ringer switch must be on for the voice and the beep.
  • Nothing leaves the phone. Roster and games are saved in this browser only. Use the roster link to move a roster to another phone.
